🌸 Places We Visit

Kaas Plateau

Explore the famous Kaas Plateau, known for its seasonal wildflowers, open landscapes and beautiful monsoon scenery. The flower season is weather-dependent.

💦 Thoseghar Waterfall

Enjoy the dramatic waterfall views and cool surroundings at Thoseghar, a popular natural attraction near Satara.

🙏 Sajjangad

Visit the historic hill fort and spiritual site of Sajjangad, associated with Sant Ramdas. Enjoy the peaceful hilltop surroundings and panoramic views.
