🌸 Day 1 — Kaas Pathar + Mahabaleshwar Temples

🌼 Kaas Plateau

Explore the famous flower valley with seasonal blooms, open landscapes and beautiful natural views. Flower season is weather-dependent.

🙏 Mahabaleshwar Temple

Visit the historic Mahabaleshwar Temple and experience its peaceful surroundings.

🛕 Krishnabai Temple

Visit Krishnabai Temple, known for its spiritual setting and connection with the Krishna River.

🛕 Shree Panchganga Temple

Visit the famous Panchganga Temple, a prominent spiritual attraction of Mahabaleshwar.

🏨 Stay at Meera Resort

Comfortable Rooms • Delicious Food • Beautiful View • Relax & Rejuvenate

Arthur's Seat

Enjoy one of Mahabaleshwar's famous scenic viewpoints.

Echo Point

Experience the popular echo point and surrounding hill views.

Venna Lake

Spend time around the scenic lake and enjoy the peaceful surroundings.

Mapro Garden

Visit the famous Mapro Garden, known for Mahabaleshwar's strawberry products.

Lodwick Point

Enjoy panoramic hill and valley views, especially around sunset.

Cascades Point

Explore the waterfall view and lush surroundings.
