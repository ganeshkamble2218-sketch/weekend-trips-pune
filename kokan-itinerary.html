🌊 Highlights of the Trip

⭐ Shrivardhan Beach – Enjoy a peaceful Konkan shoreline, fresh sea breeze and beautiful coastal views.

⭐ Aravi Beach – A quieter beach surrounded by natural beauty, ideal for relaxing and capturing scenic moments.

⭐ Shri Swarna Ganesh Mandir – Visit the famous temple and experience the spiritual side of the Konkan region.

⭐ Diveagar Beach – Spend time along the wide sandy coastline and enjoy the refreshing atmosphere of Diveagar.

⭐ Konkan Coastal Drive – Enjoy the scenic journey through lush greenery, village roads and beautiful coastal landscapes.

📝 Itinerary Description

Shrivardhan – Diveagar One Day Tour

Every Sunday | ₹1,050/- Per Person

Tour Overview

Experience the beauty of the Konkan coastline with our Shrivardhan–Diveagar One Day Tour from Pune. Explore peaceful beaches, enjoy scenic coastal views, visit the famous Swarna Ganesh Temple and spend a refreshing day away from the city.

📍 Places We Visit

1. Shrivardhan Beach
A relaxing coastal stop where you can enjoy the sea breeze, shoreline views and a peaceful Konkan atmosphere.

2. Aravi Beach
Known for its natural surroundings and quieter setting, Aravi is perfect for a refreshing beach break.

3. Shri Swarna Ganesh Mandir
A popular spiritual attraction in Diveagar, offering visitors a chance to explore the cultural and religious heritage of the region.

4. Diveagar Beach
Enjoy the open sandy beach, coastal scenery and time to relax with friends and family.

5. Konkan Coastal Drive
The journey itself is part of the experience, with greenery, village landscapes and scenic roads along the Konkan coast.
